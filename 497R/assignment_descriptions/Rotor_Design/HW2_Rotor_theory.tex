\section{Theory}
\label{sec:theory}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\roman*.]
	\item List the assumptions of BEMT. How do they affect the accuracy of the method.
	\item What are the main limitations of BEMT? Is there anything that they are dependent on that determines the accuracy of the method? Are there any cases where BEMT is not applicable?
	\item Describe the physical meaning of the axial and tangential induction factors. How do they influence the local flow around a blade element? 
	\item Why is it necessary to couple blade element theory with momentum theory in BEMT? What would be missing if you only used one of the two?
\end{enumerate}

\section{Exploration}
\label{sec:exploration}

Complete the following exploration.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\roman*.]
	\item Find experimental data for a rotor of your choice. This could be a propeller, wind turbine, or helicopter rotor. If the data is not in a form that can be used directly, you may need to digitize it using a tool like \href{https://automeris.io/WebPlotDigitizer/}{WebPlotDigitizer}.
	\item Implement the rotor in CCBlade.jl and compare the results to the experimental data. 
	\item Determine the error between the experimental data and the CCBlade.jl results. What might cause the source of this error? How could you improve the accuracy of your results?
\end{enumerate}
